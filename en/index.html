Tintei Privacy Policy

Updated November 8, 2026 · Version 3

Summary: by default Tintei works only on your device: your PDFs are opened, edited and saved there, with no account, ads or trackers. Tintei has one online feature that only works if you use it: share by link (uploads a copy of the PDF to our server). This policy explains exactly what leaves your device in that case.

1. Who we are

Tintei is a free PDF editor. Responsible party: Robert Souza Santana. Privacy contact: neuroflow.api@gmail.com.

2. What stays only on your device

  • Opening, editing text, annotating, signing, organizing pages, merging, splitting, creating PDFs, comparing, password protection and text recognition (OCR) happen on your device, inside the app. Your documents' content is not sent to anyone by these features.
  • When you tap "Download PDF", the file is handed to your system's share sheet. Where it goes is your choice.
  • Stored on the device: preferences (theme, zoom, file-name suffix), up to 3 drawn signatures (as strokes), the list of links you created (with the code to delete them), a random device identifier (see section 4) and your acceptance of the sharing notice. Uninstalling the app removes all of it.

3. Share by link (optional)

When you tap Create link, Tintei uploads a copy of the PDF (with your edits applied, up to 25 MB) to our storage, hosted by Vercel (Vercel Blob), and records the link in our database, hosted by Neon (Postgres, São Paulo region, on Amazon Web Services infrastructure).

  • Who can see it: anyone who has the link. The link code is random and long, but whoever receives it can open the document and pass it on. Don't use this feature for documents you don't want others to read. The file is not end-to-end encrypted on the server.
  • For how long: you choose 1, 7 or 30 days. After that the link stops opening and a daily cleanup deletes the file and records (within about 24 hours after expiry). You can delete the link earlier, at any time, under "Share by link" in the app.
  • Comments: if you leave them on, anyone who opens the link can comment without an account, giving a name of their choice, the text and a point in the document. Comments are visible to anyone with the link and are deleted with the link.
  • What the server stores: the file; the file name, size and dates; the link code and the deletion code only as hashes (an irreversible "fingerprint", salted with a server secret); the comments (name given, text, page, position and date); and, to limit abuse, a hash of the device identifier and a hash of the IP address of whoever created the link, commented or reported. We don't store IP addresses in clear text in our records.
  • Reports: anyone who opens a link can report the document or a comment (free-text reason). Reports from three different people take the item offline pending review. When there is a report, the person responsible for Tintei may open the reported file to assess it.
  • The providers (Vercel and Neon) also process technical connection logs (such as IP addresses) to run their infrastructure, under their own policies.

4. Device identifier

The app creates a random code on this device (it is not an advertising ID, is not linked to you and is not the device's serial number). It is sent to our server when you share, and the server keeps it only as a hash, to limit abuse (for example, 20 links per day). The limit counters are deleted after a few days.

5. Other third-party services

  • App updates (Expo / EAS Update): the app contacts Expo's service to download new versions of the code; it receives technical connection data (IP address, platform and app version), never documents. Policy: expo.dev/privacy.
  • Text recognition (OCR) files: the first time, the app downloads the engine and language files from our server (Vercel); they are kept on the device. Only those files are downloaded; your document's content never leaves the device for OCR.

6. Transfers to other countries

Vercel operates in the United States. When you use sharing, the data you chose to send is processed outside Brazil, based on your consent and that provider's contractual safeguards.

7. Legal basis and purposes

We process data with your consent (you choose to create a link and accept the notice first) and, for rate-limit records and reports, under our legitimate interest in keeping the service safe and free of abuse. We don't use data for advertising, profiling or sale.

8. What we don't do

  • We don't ask for sign-up, email, phone number or location.
  • We don't use ads, trackers, analytics cookies or metrics tools. The recipient's link page doesn't use cookies; it only keeps in the browser, if the person comments, the name they typed, to prefill next time.
  • We don't sell personal data or share it with third parties for their own purposes.

9. Drawn signatures

Tintei's signature is an image drawn on the document. It is not a digital signature with a certificate and may not carry the same legal weight as a qualified digital signature. Check that the recipient accepts this kind of signature.

10. Children

Tintei is not directed at children. The online feature (share by link) is for people aged 13 and over; minors should use it with a guardian's supervision.

11. Your rights

At any time you can delete your links in the app, and ask for information, correction or deletion of the data we process. Because we store codes as hashes, to find anything we need the document link: write to neuroflow.api@gmail.com with the link. We also handle requests to remove content that infringes rights (see the Terms of Use). You may also complain to your data protection authority (in Brazil, the ANPD).

12. Changes to this policy

If Tintei changes how it handles data, this policy will be updated first and the date above will change. The latest version is always on this page.
