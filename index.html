Tintei Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026 · Version 1

Summary: Tintei does not collect personal data. Your PDFs are opened, edited and saved on your device. There are no accounts, ads, trackers or usage analytics, and no document is sent to Tintei servers (Tintei has no server).

1. Who we are

Tintei is a free PDF editor. Responsible party: Robert Souza Santana. Privacy contact: neuroflow.api@gmail.com.

2. What happens to your files

  • The PDFs you open are read and processed only on your device, by the app itself.
  • None of your documents' content is sent to Tintei or to third parties.
  • When you tap "Download PDF", the file is handed to your system's share sheet (to save to Files, send in a message, etc.). Where it goes is your choice.

3. What is stored on your device

  • Preferences: theme, initial zoom and the suffix added to downloaded file names.
  • Signatures: up to 3 signatures you drew, stored as strokes (not as a photo).

This data stays on your device only. You can delete signatures in Settings, and uninstalling the app removes everything.

4. App updates (third-party service)

The installed app contacts the Expo (EAS Update) update service to check for and download new versions of the app's code. In doing so, the service receives technical data common to any connection, such as IP address, platform (Android or iOS) and app version. No documents or personal content are sent.

That technical data is handled under Expo's privacy policy: expo.dev/privacy.

5. What we don't do

  • We don't ask for sign-up, email, phone number or location.
  • We don't use ads, trackers, analytics cookies or metrics tools.
  • We don't sell or share personal data.

6. Drawn signatures

Tintei's signature is an image drawn on the document. It is not a digital signature with a certificate and may not carry the same legal weight as a qualified digital signature. Check that the recipient accepts this kind of signature.

7. Children

Tintei does not collect data from anyone, including children, and is not specifically directed at minors.

8. Your rights

Because we don't collect personal data, we hold none of yours to access, correct or delete. You can still write to neuroflow.api@gmail.com with any question or request about this policy or applicable privacy law (including Brazil's LGPD, Law No. 13,709/2018, and the GDPR).

9. Changes to this policy

If Tintei begins handling data differently (for example, with an optional online feature), this policy will be updated first and the effective date above will change. The latest version is always on this page.
